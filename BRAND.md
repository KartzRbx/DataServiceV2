# KeepData brand

## Concept

- **Chameleon mark** — geometric white chameleon on black (adaptability, keeping data in sync).
- **Keep** — persistence and sync; **Data** — typed stores and paths.

| Token | Hex | Use |

| Sync Cyan | `#0891b2` / `#06b6d4` | Accents, path lines |
