# KeepData brand

## Concept

- **Chameleon mark** — geometric white chameleon on black; adaptability and “keeping” data in sync.
- **Keep** — persistence and sync; **Data** — typed stores and paths.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Keep Blue | `#1d4ed8` | Primary, gradients |
| Sync Cyan | `#0891b2` / `#06b6d4` | Accents, path lines |
| Ink | `#0f172a` | Wordmark (light backgrounds) |
